Our Vision

Our long-term vision is to make AfriLink the trusted transaction layer for intra-African commerce.

We envision a future where an African business can enter a new market, find or invite a verified trading partner, agree to a transaction, secure payment, manage documentation, coordinate delivery, and complete the transaction from one platform.

The ultimate goal is not simply to process payments. It is to make trust programmable for African commerce.

Overview

Why AfriLink exists

AfriLink is a digital B2B cross-border trade infrastructure platform designed to make trading between African businesses more secure, transparent, and efficient.

The African Continental Free Trade Area (AfCFTA) is creating the policy foundation for a single African market. However, businesses still face significant practical barriers when attempting to trade across borders, particularly SMEs.

A Kenyan supplier may want to sell to a Nigerian distributor, but the two businesses may have never interacted before. They must establish trust, negotiate terms, create documentation, arrange payment, coordinate shipment, and determine when payment should be released.

Today, these activities are often fragmented across banks, payment platforms, email, WhatsApp, logistics companies, spreadsheets, and customs agents.

AfriLink aims to provide the digital transaction layer that brings these activities together.
